Privacy Policy for Stacks

Effective date: 15.10.2026

Stacks does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Comics, reading progress, reader preferences, custom artwork, and supporter badges are stored locally on the user’s device. Stacks does not upload comic files to servers operated by the developer.

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive payment card information.

If a user contacts the developer by email, the information they choose to provide will be used only to respond to that message.

Stacks contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

Users can remove individual comics from within Stacks. All locally stored app data can be removed by deleting the app.
